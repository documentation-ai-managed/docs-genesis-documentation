## Overview

Genesis offers extensive customization options to make your ebooks uniquely yours. From visual styles to content themes, you can tailor every aspect of your ebook to match your vision and brand.

## Custom Branding

For business users, integrate your brand elements into ebooks.

<ParamField body="logo" param-type="string" required="false">
  Upload your company logo for inclusion on covers and headers.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="brandColors" param-type="array" required="false">
  Specify your brand color palette as hex codes.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="brandFonts" param-type="string" required="false">
  Provide URLs to your custom brand fonts.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Custom branding is available on Pro and Enterprise plans.
</Callout>

## Interactive Elements

Add interactive features to enhance reader engagement.

- Embedded videos and audio clips
- Interactive quizzes and assessments
- Hyperlinked references and footnotes
- Animated illustrations (EPUB only)

<Expandable title="Advanced Features" default-open="false">
  Enterprise users can add custom JavaScript for enhanced interactivity.
</Expandable>
