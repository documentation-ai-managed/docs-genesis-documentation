## Getting Started

Genesis is an AI-powered learning ebook creation platform that enables users to generate high-quality, customized study ebooks across various genres and styles. With Genesis, users can describe their story idea, choose a visual style, and let the AI technology weave a magical tale. The platform offers a range of features, including children's story creation, sci-fi novel building, and brand story generation, allowing users to produce professional-looking ebooks for different audiences and purposes.

## Supported Formats

Genesis outputs ebooks in popular formats including EPUB, PDF, and MOBI. Each format is optimized for different reading devices and platforms, ensuring your content looks professional on e-readers, tablets, and smartphones.

<Expandable title="View Format Details" default-open="false">
  - **EPUB**: Best for e-readers like Kindle, supports interactive elements
  - **PDF**: Universal compatibility, ideal for print-on-demand services
  - **MOBI**: Optimized for older Kindle devices
