## Overview

Creating ebooks with Genesis is a straightforward process that combines your creative input with our advanced AI technology. Whether you're a first-time author or an experienced writer, our platform guides you through each step to produce professional-quality ebooks.

## Getting Started

Before creating your first ebook, ensure you have a clear idea of your content. Genesis works best when you provide detailed descriptions, but even simple concepts can yield excellent results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user">
    Create your Genesis account and verify your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Dashboard" icon="layout">
    Log in to your dashboard and click "Create New Ebook".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="edit">
    Provide your ebook title, description, and target audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Input Requirements

Genesis requires minimal input to generate comprehensive ebooks. The more detail you provide, the better the results.

<ParamField path="title" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your ebook title (up to 100 characters).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="description" param-type="string" required="true">
  Detailed description of your story or content (minimum 200 words).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="genre" param-type="string" required="false">
  Choose from children's, sci-fi, romance, mystery, etc.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="audience" param-type="string" required="false">
  Specify age group or target readers.
</ParamField>

## Best Practices

To achieve the best results with Genesis, follow these guidelines.

- Provide detailed descriptions with specific character traits and plot elements
- Choose appropriate genres that match your target audience
- Review and edit generated content for personal touches
- Use high-quality reference images for visual styles

<Callout kind="tip">
  Experiment with different genres and styles to discover what works best for your content.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during ebook creation, try these solutions.

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Generation failed | Check your description length and try again |
| Poor quality | Provide more specific details in your input |
| Formatting issues | Use the preview editor to adjust layout |
